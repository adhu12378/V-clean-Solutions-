# VClean Solutions premium landing page

## Overview
Build a polished, single-page cleaning-services website that uses the supplied company details and pricing exactly. The page will combine strong visual proof, interactive service discovery, postcode coverage checking, and a complete instant-quote booking journey.

## Page experience
- Add a sticky glass navigation bar with the VClean brand, section links, phone chip, and quote button.
- Create an animated opening section with the supplied headline, supporting copy, trust indicators, four headline stats, three cleaning photographs, and two booking actions.
- Add a concise “Why VClean” band covering vetted cleaners, eco-safe products, the re-clean guarantee, clear pricing, and flexible scheduling.
- Present all eight services in a responsive horizontal slider/grid. Each service opens a detailed checklist with recommended frequency and can preselect itself in the quote form.
- Build a draggable before/after comparison with four selectable room/workspace presets.
- Add a postcode coverage checker for the supplied Bedfordshire, Buckinghamshire, and Hertfordshire examples, returning coverage tier and estimated arrival details.
- Create a flowing testimonials section with the five named reviewers, five-star ratings, locations, service labels, and natural review copy.
- Build the full six-part quote and booking calculator in one clear flow: service, size/hours, add-ons, postcode/address, date/time, and contact details. Prices update instantly; submission opens a confirmation summary and notification.
- Finish with complete contact details, hours, service areas, direct call/email actions, and social proof.

## Visual direction
- Deep teal foundations, crisp white surfaces, mint/emerald accents, and warm gold review stars.
- Editorial, premium typography with strong hierarchy; restrained glass effects and compact corners.
- High-quality cleaning photography stored with the project rather than external hotlinks.
- Subtle entrance, hover, floating, counter, slider, and marquee motion, with reduced-motion support.
- Fully responsive navigation, imagery, comparisons, cards, and form controls for desktop and mobile.

## Functional details
- Navigation and CTAs smoothly scroll to the correct page sections.
- Service selection carries into the calculator automatically.
- Quote total is calculated from hourly rate × selected estimated hours plus add-ons.
- Postcode matching is handled in-page for the requested coverage examples and surrounding listed regions; unsupported entries receive a clear contact prompt.
- Booking submission is a polished front-end confirmation experience; no email delivery or persistent booking storage is included.
- Add route-specific search/social metadata for VClean Solutions and accessible labels, keyboard states, image text, and semantic structure.

## Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts in the running preview.
- Exercise service details, quote prefill, before/after dragging, postcode checks, total calculations, and confirmation flow.
- Confirm there are no visual overlaps or browser errors.
